About

I’m a Computer Engineer focused on translating algorithms and architectures into efficient silicon.

My experience spans ASIC/RTL design, cache and coherence, high‑speed SRAM, and RISC‑V extensions for ML acceleration. I enjoy making pragmatic micro‑architectural trade‑offs to hit power, performance, and area targets.

  • Hands‑on with SystemVerilog/Verilog and verification‑driven RTL
